Cadence: 2-minute demo script (impact-focused)

[0:00–0:30] The problem

"Think about the best class you ever took. Now think about the worst. The material was probably the same. The difference was the teacher.

"Every semester, thousands of TAs and new teachers stand in front of a room with no idea how they come across. They practice alone, in the mirror or in their head. Nobody's there to say 'you lost them at minute three,' or 'you never explained the one concept the exam depends on.' Mentors who can give that feedback are rare. So people learn to teach by teaching real students, and the students pay for it in their grades and their confidence. "

[0:30–0:45] The solution

"We built Cadence so TAs and instructors get feedback before they ever face a student. Upload your lecture materials, teach out loud, and get a review grounded in what you actually said and what you were supposed to cover. Here it is live."

[0:45–1:25] Live demo

[Screen recording. A uploads a slide deck and the key

“A TA uploads their deck or lecture notes, and Cadence pulls out the key concepts students should walk away understanding"

[click start]

"Now they teach, and the pace, filler, and pitch meters updates more than three times a second, measured from the audio."

[click finish. The review screen loads.]

"Onto the review. It shows the exact quote from the session, what happened, why it matters, and a line to try instead. It also flags that when a core concept is not covered.
That's the kind of gap that turns into a wrong answer on an exam. "

[1:25–1:50] Why it's not another speech coach

"Speech-coaching apps optimize the speaker: fewer ums, better pace. We do that, and more. Students struggle when a concept is skipped, when there's no example, when nobody checks whether they understood. Cadence coaches the teaching, not just the speech.

And why not open a chatbot or another speech coach app and talk to it? A chatbot doesn't know what you were supposed to teach, can’t tell you when you asked a question and didn’t give students enough time to think before giving the answer, and it can't measure your pace. It will also happily invent feedback. Every Cadence critique has to cite a quote we verify in the transcript. If we can't cite it, we don't say it. That's the difference between flattery and coaching."

[1:50–2:00] close

"Every hour a TA practices with Cadence is an hour that student doesn't spend confused in the back row. Better-prepared teachers mean students learn more and stay engaged, and the students get the teacher they deserve. Thank you."
